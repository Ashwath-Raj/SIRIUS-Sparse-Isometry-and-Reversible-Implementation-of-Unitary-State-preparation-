## Scope of validation

The local smoke validates four methods on identical ordered support and coefficients, with one seed. A separate requested GPU lane records its unavailable device status. It is not a full benchmark campaign, publication reproduction, statevector validation at scale, or quantum hardware run. See `FINAL_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` and the immutable run data under `research/phase9_repair/runs/`.
